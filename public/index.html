Oct 2025 Float Formula:
1. Per input: norm = (float − skinMin) / (skinMax − skinMin)
2. avgNorm = sum(norm) / 10
3. outputFloat = (outMax − outMin) × avgNorm + outMin

Why float matters: Steam Market prices are the same for all FT skins regardless of exact float. But a FT skin at float 0.151 (bottom of FT range) normalizes to nearly 0 → can produce Minimal Wear or even Factory New output. That's the profitable trade-up scenario.
